# Privacy

Ubuntu Files Cloud Backup runs only on your computer. It has no server and no analytics, and the developer receives no data.

- **What it asks Google for:** access to files this app creates in your Google Drive (`drive.file` scope) and your email address, only to show which account is connected.
- **What it does with it:** copies the folders you choose from your computer into a folder named `Ubuntu Files Cloud Backup` in your own Drive.
- **Where the login is stored:** in `~/.config/gdrive-folder-backup/` on your computer, readable only by your user.
- **Sharing:** your data is not shared with anyone. It goes only between your computer and Google.
- **Removing access:** choose Sign out in the app, or remove the app at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Files already in Drive stay until you delete them.
